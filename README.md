# Hi, I'm Sanjalee 👋

**Computer Science and IT & Management undergraduate, working towards business analysis and quality assurance.**

I'm reading for two degrees in parallel — a BSc (Hons) in Information Technology & Management at the **University of Moratuwa** and a BSc (Hons) in Computer Science at the **Informatics Institute of Technology**. One side teaches me how systems get built; the other, how businesses decide what to build.

That combination is what pulled me towards **business analysis and QA**. In my project work I'm usually the person mapping entities and relationships before anyone opens an editor, writing the acceptance criteria, and testing the thing at the end — and I'm equally comfortable talking through a database schema with someone who has never seen one.

Off-screen I play hockey for my university, and previously for the Sri Lanka Schools team.
